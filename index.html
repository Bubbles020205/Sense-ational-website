Our story

A little curiosity goes a long way.

Sense-ational brings together sensory discovery, natural ingredients, creativity and interactive fun in one unforgettable experience.

More than just a market stall.

Our idea is simple: everyday experiences become extraordinary when you take the time to explore them through your senses.

From the feel of a sugar body scrub to the aroma of botanical bracelets and the flavour of herbal iced tea, each experience invites you to slow down, discover something new and have fun.

We also bring the senses to life through interactive sight and sound challenges, turning curiosity into a playful experience for everyone.
