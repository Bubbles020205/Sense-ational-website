More Than Just a Market

Sense-ational Market Day is an interactive experience built around the five human senses: touch, taste, smell, sight and sound.

Each stall offers a different activity or product, giving visitors the opportunity to explore, participate and have fun while learning about the world around them.

Whether you're creating your own body scrub, tasting herbal iced tea or taking on one of our games, there's something to discover at every stall.

Visit all five stalls, complete your digital stamp card and qualify for our special Tea Bomb promotion!

🍬 What Is a Tea Bomb?

A Tea Bomb is a hard candy shell containing loose-leaf tea, herbs and dried edible flowers. Place it in a mug or teapot and add hot water. As the shell melts or cracks, the contents are released into the water, creating a colourful, aromatic tea that is lightly sweetened by the shell.
