Meet the project

About Sense-ational

A student-led bioentrepreneurship project that brings science, nature and sensory discovery together.

Our Story

Sense-ational was created to make the five senses part of a fun, interactive experience. Our concept connects everyday sensory experiences with herbs, natural ingredients and creative activities.

From handmade body scrubs and herbal iced tea to memory and coordination challenges, each stall offers a different way to explore the world around us.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create an engaging sensory experience that encourages discovery, creativity and appreciation for nature.

We want every visitor to learn something, have fun and leave with a memorable Sense-ational experience.

Discover the challenge

Put your memory and observation skills to the test.

How to play:

  • Identify the card that was removed.
  • Progress through the challenge.
