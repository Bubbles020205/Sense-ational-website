Sight — Herb Memory Game

Think you've got a sharp eye and an excellent memory? Put yourself to the test!

How to Play

  1. Study 10–15 herb cards for 20 seconds.
  2. One card is removed.
  3. Identify the missing card to progress.
  4. Each successful round removes more cards.
  5. Earn 5 points for every round progressed.

Your name, contact number and score will be recorded. The highest-scoring player at the end of Market Day wins a big prize!

Entry: R10 per attempt.
Enter as many times as you like to try to improve your score. Game entries cannot be pre-ordered.

Sound — Wire Buzzing Challenge

Steady hands, sharp concentration and nerves of steel. Can you beat the buzzer?

Round 1

  • Complete the wire course in 30 seconds.
  • Start with 15 points.
  • Lose 5 points for each buzz.

Round 2

  • Advance only if you finish Round 1 without buzzing.
  • Complete the course in 20 seconds.
  • Start with another 15 points and lose 5 per buzz.

Round 3

  • Advance only if you finish Round 2 without buzzing.
  • Complete the course in 10 seconds.
  • Start with another 15 points and lose 5 per buzz.

Your total score will be recorded with your name and contact number. The highest-scoring player wins a big prize!

Entry: R10 per attempt.
Enter as many times as you like. Game entries cannot be pre-ordered.

🫖 Meet the Tea Bomb

A little burst of tea magic!

What is a Tea Bomb?

A Tea Bomb is a hard-candy shell containing loose-leaf tea, herbs or dried edible flowers. When added to hot water, the shell melts or cracks open, releasing its contents and lightly sweetening your drink.

How Does It Work?

  1. Drop the Tea Bomb into a mug or teapot.
  2. Pour hot or boiling water over it.
  3. Watch the shell dissolve or crack open.
  4. Allow the tea and botanicals to infuse, then enjoy!
